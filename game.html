roamers live in family groups with needs: they graze, walk in file, carry stones to cairns at the boss-ring pillars (and pieces of any tree that's come down near them), and lie down together. knock a cairn over, fly into one, or let the monkey's bombnanas land near them and they remember who did it: the bold ones square up and headbutt, the rest keep away. grudges fade over minutes. lurkers stand at the forest's edge passing for firs, and only move or turn their heads while you're not looking; they learn where you spend your time and reposition to watch. colossals keep a minutes-long routine between the pillars and spires and don't care about you. everyone looks at whatever is most interesting nearby, you and the monkey included, then gets bored of it. emus go quiet near a colossal, give lurkers a wide berth, and ignore roamers unless there's a grudge. the clock: at dawn and dusk each family goes to its own pillar's cairns, at night they sleep beside them; at dusk two that have spent long enough together take two tree pieces off their cairn, bow either side of it, and a young one is there (it grows up over a few minutes). lurkers shift more in the dark; emus roost and lay at night, and a bolting emu goes straight through thin trees. the banana button (above the right pad) throws a banana where you look: everything wants a look at it. ink ties: draw a little circle on one thing, a line, and a little circle on another, and they're tied (a circle on open ground pins it there); a blade swipe across a tie cuts it. attention lines: amber emus, blue roamers, white colossals, red lurkers.

MOVE 動

  • left pad: move · push forward for gas
  • right pad: drag to look · tap to grapple · hold still to fire the rope (let go to drop) · swipe to slash
  • ⚔ blade / ✎ pen: the pen draws signs instead of slashing
  • banana throws one where you look · ride hops on a nearby emu · emote · watch replays the last seconds
  • UI tab, bottom left: one subject at a time; the world holds still while it's open
  • ⌂ tab, left edge: the hub (also the red gate in your starting clearing)
  • J tab, under ⌂: your journal. What you live through gets jotted down; a lurker keeps it for good
  • sound: on/off at the top of the UI deck, volumes under UI → view

  • sit up on a spire at dusk and wait
  • find a lurker (a fir with a face) and stand still where you can see it: it keeps your journal
